# N. Short imperative title of the decision

One short paragraph stating the decision itself, in the present tense and in bold where it matters —
what a reader needs even if they read nothing else. Not the background; the decision.

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR-XXXX](./XXXX-...md)
- Decided in: #<issue> (and the wayfinding tickets or spec sections that produced it)
- Bears on: the PRD sections or other ADRs this touches

## Context

What forces are in play — the measured facts, the constraints, the thing that makes this a decision
rather than a default. Enough that the reader understands *why* a choice was necessary.

## Decision

The decision, precisely, with the exact values, pins, or rules an implementer must follow.

## Alternatives considered

Each real alternative and why it was not taken. "Rejected on measurement, not taste" beats a bare
assertion. If an alternative stays available as a fallback, say so.

## Consequences

What this commits the project to, including the costs knowingly accepted. Name the ones you dislike —
a future reader will find them anyway, and finding them here reads as deliberate rather than missed.
