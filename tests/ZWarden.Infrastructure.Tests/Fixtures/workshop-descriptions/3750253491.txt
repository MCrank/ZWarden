The update for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2875848298]Common Sense by BitBraven[/url] is finally here!

There are so many half-baked patches and updates that either cut out a bunch of features or straight out don't work... Is it that hard to fully update a mod like this? [b]I don't think so.[/b] So here's my attempt to fully revive our beloved mod.

[img]https://i.imgur.com/RMNv3Ur.png[/img]
[list]
[*] 1. Pry open Doors, Windows and Vehicles with Crowbars (reinforced doors cannot be opened by default, look at config opts).
[*] 2. Collect Garbage, Materials, etc. off the ground.
[*] 3. (new) Reload all selected magazines at once.
[*] 4. Attach hand Flashlights to the toolbelt.
[*] 5. Gun Ammo Counter on the Top Left of the screen.
[*] 6. Use any scissors or screwdrivers to open canned food (can get injured).
[*] 7. (new) Replace dirty bandages.
[*] 8. Equip items right from the ground.
[*] 9. Wash only equipped or unequipped items.
[*] 10. Make Sheets with improvised materials.
[*] 11. Use Alcohol to sanitize Dirty Bandages.
[*] 12. Easier fuel transfer in campfires, bbqs, fireplaces...
[/list]
[b]Features already in vanilla:[/b]
[list]
[*] Collect forest goods from the ground.
[*] Repair various weapons and tools with glue.
[*] Highlight vehicle's engine, battery and fuel condition in the dashboard.
[*] Pour the contents of containers into the ground.
[*] Wash only weapons.
[/list]
